I built an AI-powered platform to help advertisers orchestrate, observe, and optimize campaigns across channels.

The hardest part wasn’t the AI—it was the real world:

* messy data (different APIs, different schemas),
* missing instrumentation,
* brittle workflows that lived in spreadsheets,
* and no single source of truth for performance monitoring.

## What I shipped

* A unified dashboard for cross-channel analytics and workflow creation
* Observability: KPI tracking and lifecycle metrics to guide iteration
* Monetization experiments (freemium + usage-based)
* Measurement: activation, engagement, retention, revenue signals

## Lessons learned

* Observability is a product feature, not an afterthought.
* Pricing is a hypothesis—treat it like an experiment.
* Velocity comes from alignment and scope management, not heroics.
